**🫀 Smoking Health Risk Analysis Dashboard**

This interactive Power BI dashboard is designed to analyze the relationship between smoking habits and health risks across different organs and patient demographics. The dashboard provides a visual and interactive way to understand how smoking-related risk varies based on smoking status, gender, age group, smoking duration, and daily cigarette intake.

**🎯 Project Objective**

The objective of this project is to transform health and smoking-related data into an interactive analytical dashboard 
that allows users to explore health risks from multiple perspectives and understand patterns across organs, age groups, gender, and smoking behavior.


**🔄 Interactive Organ & Health Analysis**

The dashboard includes interactive “Damaged” and “Healthy” buttons. When a user selects either option, the dashboard dynamically updates the displayed organ and related health-risk information according to the selected condition.

The left-side navigation allows users to explore different organs, including:

🫀 Heart
🧍 Human Body
🫘 Kidney
🫁 Liver
🫁 Lungs

This makes the dashboard useful for comparing organ-specific health conditions rather than showing only overall smoking statistics.


**📊 Key Dashboard Insights:**


- Patient Count: Displays the number of patients associated with the selected health-risk condition.
  
- Smoking Status: Shows the percentage/count of patients who are Never, Current, or Former smokers.
  
- Smoking Status by Gender: Compares smoking patterns between male and female patients.
  
- Smoking Duration & Daily Intake: Analyzes the relationship between years of smoking and cigarettes consumed per day across different age groups.
  
- Cholesterol & Hypertension Risk: Shows the distribution of High, Low, and Normal risk levels across different age groups.
  
- Age Group Analysis: Provides insights across groups such as 18–28, 29–38, 39–48, 49–58, 59–68, and 69+.
  
- Dynamic Filtering: Visuals and KPIs change based on the selected health condition and organ.
